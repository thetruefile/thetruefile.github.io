The Man Who Survived Both Atomic Bombs

Case file: Tsutomu Yamaguchi, Hiroshima and Nagasaki, August 1945

Watch the Short

Hiroshima

On August 6, 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi was 29 years old, an engineer on a business trip to Hiroshima. At 8:15 in the morning the first atomic bomb exploded over the city. The blast threw him to the ground. He was badly burned, half blind and his eardrums were broken.

The train home

He spent the night in a shelter. Then he did the only thing he could think of: he went home, to Nagasaki, by train.

Nagasaki

Back at work, bandaged, he told his boss what he had seen: one bomb, one whole city gone. His boss said he was crazy, since nobody could destroy a city with one bomb. Then, at 11:02 on August 9, the window turned white. The second bomb had fallen on Nagasaki.

What happened next

Yamaguchi lived to 93. In 2009 the Japanese government officially recognized him as a survivor of both bombings. He died in 2010.
